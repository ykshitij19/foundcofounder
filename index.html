Context

What is casting, and who is this for?

Casting means selecting actors for ads, films, and web series. A brand tells a casting agency what they need — the agency's job is to find matching actors and send their photos/videos to the client.

ARC is built for casting agencies — the people in the middle who manage hundreds of actors and fulfill briefs daily. They are our primary user.

  • 1
    Client gives brief e.g. "Female, 20–25, bubbly, North Indian"
  • 2
    Agency finds matching actors From their database of hundreds or thousands
  • 3
    Agency sends photos/videos Formatted as a deck or PPT, via WhatsApp or Drive
  • 4
    Client gives feedback → repeat This loop happens multiple times per casting

Storage Chaos

Thousands of actors, each with multiple files — photos, videos, PDFs — scattered across different Drive folders, hard disks, and WhatsApp chats. No structure, no single place.

Three-Tool Nightmare

Excel for data. Drive for files. WhatsApp for sending. Three separate tools that don't talk to each other, stitched together by human memory and manual effort.

Iteration = Start Over

Every time the client says "send more options" or "make it younger," the agency redoes the entire process from scratch. There is no state saved anywhere.

4 Hours. Every Casting.

One brief can take 3–4 hours end to end. A casting agency handles multiple briefs per week. This is dozens of hours of pure manual overhead — that's the business we're fixing.

Full System

How ARC works

The system has three distinct phases. Each one maps to a real pain point the agency faces today.

1
Part 1
Data In
Actor sends data (same as today) Photos, videos, PDFs, text — all via WhatsApp. Zero behavior change required from actors.
Agency forwards to ARC bot One forward message to the ARC WhatsApp number. That's the only new action.
ARC receives everything Images, videos, documents, and unstructured text all land in one pipeline.
2
Part 2
Backend Magic
File classification System separates photos, videos, and documents automatically.
Data extraction AI reads text and PDFs to extract name, age, height, skills, and contact info.
Profile created Each actor gets a structured database entry + a hosted profile page with their media.
3
Part 3
Casting Done
Brief enters ARC Agency types naturally ("male, 25–30, urban") or uses filter UI.
Search + Select ARC shows matching profiles. Agent picks 5–20. Removes or adds easily.
Generate + Send One click → PDF or PPT deck with photos, names, details. Share as link or file.
Iteration = 30 seconds Client says "more"? Change selection, click generate. No repeat work.
